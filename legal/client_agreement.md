CLIENT SERVICE AGREEMENT
Einst Personnel Management Services (EPMS) Ltd
1. Role of EPMS
EPMS operates as a service-matching platform connecting clients with independent artisans. EPMS does not directly perform services.
2. Independent Contractors
All artisans are independent contractors and are responsible for work quality, tools, materials, and safety.
3. Job Requests
When a job request is submitted, details are shared with suitable artisans. Jobs are accepted on a first-come-first-serve basis.
4. Client Responsibilities
Clients agree to provide accurate information, safe access to property, treat artisans respectfully, and pay agreed charges on time.
5. Payments
Payment terms are agreed between client and artisan or via the platform. EPMS is not responsible for payment disputes.
6. Cancellations
Clients must inform artisans promptly if cancelling. Late cancellations may incur charges.
7. Quality of Work
EPMS does not guarantee work quality. Any issues must be resolved directly with the artisan.
8. Liability
EPMS is not liable for work defects, property damage, injuries, delays, or losses. Responsibility lies with the artisan.
9. Safety
Clients must ensure a safe working environment.
10. Data Use
Client details may be shared with artisans for service delivery.
11. Agreement Acceptance
By submitting a job request, the client agrees to these terms.
