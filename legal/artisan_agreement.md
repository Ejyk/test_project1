ARTISAN SERVICE AGREEMENT
Einst Personnel Management Services (EPMS) Ltd
1. Independent Contractor Status
Artisans registered on the EPMS platform operate as independent contractors and are not employees of EPMS.
2. Services
Artisans agree to provide professional services in their registered trade and maintain required qualifications, tools, and insurance.
3. Job Acceptance
Jobs are offered on a first-come-first-serve basis. Accepting a job creates an agreement between the artisan and the client.
4. Conduct
Artisans must act professionally, respect clients, and comply with all laws and safety standards.
5. Payments & Commission
EPMS may charge commissions or lead fees. Artisans agree not to bypass platform fees.
6. Cancellations
Artisans must inform clients promptly if unable to attend.
7. Liability
Artisans are responsible for work quality, safety, and damages arising from their services.
8. Account Suspension
EPMS may suspend accounts for misconduct or policy violations.
9. Data Use
Artisan information may be shared with clients for service coordination.
10. Agreement Acceptance
By registering or accepting jobs, artisans agree to these terms.
