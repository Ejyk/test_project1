COMMISSION & FEE POLICY
Einst Personnel Management Services (EPMS) Ltd
1. Role of EPMS
EPMS operates as a service-matching platform connecting clients with independent artisans.
2. Commission Structure
EPMS may earn through per-job commissions, lead fees, or subscriptions.
3. When Fees Apply
Fees apply when a job is accepted and connection occurs via EPMS.
4. Payment Responsibility
Artisans agree not to bypass platform fees.
5. Client Charges
Clients may pay platform or processing fees where applicable.
6. Payment Methods
Payments may be collected via bank transfer, online payments, or invoicing.
7. Refunds
Refunds may apply for errors or cancelled jobs before engagement.
8. Transparency
All fees will be disclosed clearly.
9. Changes
EPMS may update commission rates with notice.
10. Agreement
Use of the platform indicates agreement to this policy.
